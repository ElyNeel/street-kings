Street Kings privacy

Last updated 25 September 2026

Street Kings is a free game by Neel Ely. You can play it without signing in. If you choose to sign in with Google, this page explains what is stored and why.

What we store

  • Your email address and Google account ID, which Google shares when you sign in. They are used only to recognise you. They are never shown to other players.
  • The racer name you pick. It is shown on the leaderboards.
  • Your best time on each route and the car you used. These are shown on the leaderboards.
  • The ghost of each best time: where your car was on the road during that run, so other players can race against it.
  • Your saved career (money, cars, upgrades and progress), so it follows you between devices.

We do not store your Google password, we do not read your emails or contacts, and we do not use advertising or tracking.

Where it is kept

Everything is stored with Supabase on servers in London. The game website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

Sign in, go to the title screen and press Delete account. Your racer name, your times, your ghosts and your saved career are removed straight away. The copy of your career on your own device stays unless you start a new one.
