How to play

The rules of the road

Getting around

The menu bar has five buttons: Race for your next story race, Street for quick races and people who want a race, Garage, Dealership and Map. On a phone it sits at the bottom of the screen. Once Essex opens, London, Essex and Mauritius sit at the top: tap one to go there. Island cars stay on the island, so flying there or back swaps the car you race.

Controls

  • ← or A move one lane left
  • → or D move one lane right
  • Hold Space for nitro
  • Esc or P to pause
  • On a phone, use the LEFT, NITRO and RIGHT buttons

Traffic

Your car accelerates by itself. Traffic comes at you in waves of one or two cars, so there is always at least one open lane. Watch the horizon, pick the gap and move early. Night buses hide the road behind them, so give them room.

Crashes

A crash slows you down and adds 8 to 10% damage. One crash will not end your race, but at 100% the car is wrecked and you lose. Damage stays on the car until you repair it.

Nitro

Hold nitro for a big burst of speed. It drains fast and refills slowly while you are not using it. If it runs dry it needs a quarter of a tank before it fires again.

Career

Eleven rivals across East and North London, then the King. Every career win pays cash and reputation once. Reputation unlocks faster cars at Rep 3, 6 and 11, and the traffic gets busier as you climb.

Familiar faces

Keep racing on the streets and word gets around. Some people will want a race with you. They show up on the Street page, under Challengers, when they are ready. A red dot on Street means someone new is waiting.

Street races

Stuck or short of cash? Street races on the Street page can be run again and again. They pay every win, and the harder ones pay more.

Garage

Every upgrade has three stages, and how much each stage adds depends on the car. Small cars like the Civic run out of room early, so you will need something with more potential for North London. In the Body Shop, spoilers, body kits and lowered suspension change top speed, nitro speed and handling. Everything you customise also raises your style rating, and style adds up to 25% to your prize money.

Checkpoints

Gold gates light up one lane now and then. Drive through them for cash and a splash of nitro, and hit them in a row to multiply the cash up to four times. A Checkpoint Run has no rival: go through every gate to win, miss one and you are out.

Stars

Every career route has three stars: one for the win, one for no crashes and one for hitting every gate. Go back and collect the ones you missed.

Essex and pink slips

Take the crown and beat Ishaaq, and the border opens. Essex has eight rivals, faster cars and pink slips: once you have beaten someone, they will race you for their car if you put yours up. Win and it is yours. Lose and they keep the car you raced.

Paused

Engine running

Quitting counts as a loss. No prize, and any damage from this race stays on the car.
